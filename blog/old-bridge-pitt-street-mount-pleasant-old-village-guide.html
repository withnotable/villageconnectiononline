The Old Bridge at Pitt Street: Mount Pleasant's Hidden Gem in the Historic Old Village

May 19th 2026

When I tell people about Mount Pleasant's best-kept secrets, the Old Bridge at Pitt Street always tops my list. Tucked away in the charming Old Village of Mount Pleasant, this historic pier offers some of the most breathtaking views and peaceful moments you'll find anywhere in the Lowcountry.

As someone who specializes in residential real estate throughout Mount Pleasant, I've walked countless clients through the Old Village neighborhoods, and without fail, when we reach the Old Bridge, they're absolutely captivated. There's something magical about this place that captures the essence of what makes Old Mount Pleasant so special.

The Rich History Behind Mount Pleasant's Old Bridge

The Pitt Street Bridge, affectionately known as the Old Bridge by locals, has deep roots in Mount Pleasant's maritime history. Originally built as a working pier, it served the bustling shrimp and fishing industry that defined Old Village life for generations. While commercial fishing boats no longer dock here regularly, the bridge maintains its authentic character and connection to Mount Pleasant's seafaring past.

Walking out onto the Old Bridge feels like stepping back in time. The weathered wooden planks and sturdy railings tell the story of countless tides, storms, and quiet mornings when local fishermen would head out before dawn. This piece of living history sits right in the heart of the Old Village, making it an integral part of what draws so many people to call this corner of Mount Pleasant home.

Why the Old Bridge is Perfect for Walking, Running, and Fishing

I love recommending the Old Bridge to my clients because it offers something for everyone. Early risers can catch spectacular sunrises over Charleston Harbor, while evening strollers are treated to some of the most romantic sunsets you'll see anywhere in Mount Pleasant. The pier extends well into the water, providing unobstructed views in every direction.

For runners, the Old Bridge offers a peaceful alternative to busy Mount Pleasant streets. The wooden surface provides a gentle, forgiving run with ocean breezes and stunning water views as motivation. I've seen joggers start their day here countless times, and there's something about running over the water that makes it feel less like exercise and more like meditation.

Fishing enthusiasts absolutely love the Old Bridge. The pier's location provides excellent access to deep water where redfish, trout, and flounder are regularly caught. Local anglers often gather here in the early morning or late afternoon, creating a wonderful sense of community that reflects the friendly spirit of Old Mount Pleasant.

Living Near Mount Pleasant's Old Village and the Old Bridge

When clients ask me about the appeal of Old Village properties, the Old Bridge is always part of the conversation. Imagine having this peaceful retreat just a short walk from your front door. The neighborhoods surrounding the Old Village offer charming historic homes, many with traditional Lowcountry architecture that perfectly complements the area's maritime character.

Properties near the Old Bridge tend to hold their value exceptionally well, and it's easy to understand why. The combination of historic charm, waterfront access, and that small-town feel within Mount Pleasant creates a unique living experience. As Jackie Kelly, I've helped numerous families find their perfect home in these Old Village neighborhoods, and the proximity to the Old Bridge is often a deciding factor.

The Old Bridge also serves as a gateway to exploring more of Mount Pleasant's waterfront. From here, you can see across to Charleston's historic skyline, watch dolphins play in the harbor, and truly appreciate the natural beauty that makes this area so desirable for both visitors and residents.

Discovering More of Old Mount Pleasant's Character

The Old Bridge perfectly represents what I love about helping people find homes in Mount Pleasant. It's not just about the property itself – it's about the lifestyle, the community, and those special places that make you fall in love with where you live. The Old Village, with the Old Bridge as its crown jewel, offers that rare combination of history, natural beauty, and small-town charm that's increasingly hard to find.

Whether you're considering a move to Mount Pleasant or you're already here and haven't discovered this gem yet, I encourage you to visit the Old Bridge at Pitt Street. Take a morning walk, bring your fishing gear, or simply sit and watch the sunset. You'll quickly understand why this historic pier holds such a special place in the hearts of Old Village residents.
